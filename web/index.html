Data quality & interpretation

Exact duplicate source rows are removed. Other same-invoice/product lines are retained. Missing CustomerID contributes to ledger sales but never to cohorts. Invalid fields, zero price and negative price are excluded from monetary metrics. Positive cancellation anomalies cannot be purchases. First observed purchase is not necessarily customer acquisition. No original-invoice link, costs or profit are available.
